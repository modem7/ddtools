# Blueprint: party handout

## Purpose and audience

A short, friendly introduction the player can give every teammate
(`audience: party`): who the character is **as the party sees them**, how to play
off them, and what they bring to a fight. Keep it short: as long as it needs to be, no
longer (about two pages; a new character's may be one).

## Never include

- Anything in `secrets.txt`, or anything that hints at it: catchphrases that match a
  secret alias, "out of character" promises that reveal the act ("the cowardice is just
  an act", "he always turns up in the end"), jokes about a hidden past.
- Relationship spoilers between characters that the table may want to play out
  (Mossy: a secret rivalry with the party's cook). Keep teammate lines tactical.
- Backstory beyond what the party has read (e.g. only the short backstory).
- Spells the character doesn't have yet, unless labelled with their level or listed
  under Coming Soon.

## Sections

- [h2] What You'll Notice About Them
  A two-column table of visible traits. Open the page with the title, a one-line
  subtitle and an intro box built only from the public backstory.
- [h2] A Note From the Player
  Out of character, three bullets: they're built to be played against; they have
  growing to do and the party can be part of it; if a bit stops being fun, say so.
  Nothing that explains away the character's act.
- [h2] Easy Ways to Play Off Them
  Things teammates can do or ask to start a scene.
- [h2] Running Gag
  The public face of the character's bit (Mossy: her star ratings for every meal the
  party eats, read out loud).
- [h1] In a Fight
  Role in one sentence.
- [h2] What They Can Do for You Right Now
  Only current abilities, as "what it means for you".
- [h2] Coming Soon
  Level → what the party gets. The spell check allows future spells here only.
- [h2] Working Together
  One tactical bullet per teammate, plus the party's recurring battlefield.

## Rules learned

- Reread it as a teammate would, hunting for hints, before sending.
- Any numbers (prices, damage, distances) are sized for the party's current level.
- Also apply every rule in [README.md](README.md).

## QA checklist

- [ ] `ddtools check <dir>` passes (spoilers and spells).
- [ ] No wasted space, no stray last page, no heading stranded at a page bottom.
- [ ] Every sentence is something the party already knows or can see.
