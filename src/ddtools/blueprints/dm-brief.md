# Blueprint: DM brief

## Purpose and audience

Everything the DM needs to run the character well (`audience: dm`): who they are,
their secrets, the NPCs and hooks, how the build affects balance, and the open rules
questions. Written as ideas, not requests.

## Never include

- Nothing is secret from the DM, but keep other players' secrets out: this is about
  this character.
- Don't pad it: the DM reads several of these. As long as it needs to be, no longer
  (a well-developed one is about five pages).

## Sections

- [h2] At a Glance
  Table: concept, at the table, underneath, role, build, the player's goals (including
  what the player finds hard, so hooks give the character something to react to).
  Open with a "How to use this" box: ideas not requests; "Secret" marks what the
  party doesn't know.
- [h2] What the Party Knows
  What the party has read, and a box on how the party sees the character, including
  what the player wants to reveal in play (so NPCs don't give it away).
- [h2] What the Party Doesn't Know (Secret)
  Table: secret, detail, good reveal moment.
- [h1] Their Story
  One paragraph.
- [h2] Key NPCs
  NPC, role, what they want from the character.
- [h2] Setting Details Worth Using
  Lore the DM can pull on.
- [h2] Their Arc
  Stage, what it looks like, what pushes it.
- [h2] Lines & Limits
  What the player would rather avoid; `[placeholder]` until they say.
- [h1] Hooks
  Grouped by theme; mark party-sensitive groups "(Secret)". Include any rule the
  player follows that the DM can use (Mossy: she never admits a meal was good).
- [h1] Mechanics
  How the build affects the table.
- [h2] Current Numbers
  Stats grid for the current level.
- [h2] Level Plan
  What the DM will see at each level, and a loot wishlist.
- [h2] Rules & Rulings
  From `campaign.rules` (`show_in` keeps guide-only rows out).
- [h2] Blanks for You to Fill
  Names and details the DM owns.

## Rules learned

- Be honest about balance: show the numbers for any engine the build runs (e.g. how
  many extra resources a week a rest-based trick really yields under the table's rules).
- Separate confirmed rulings from working assumptions.
- Also apply every rule in [README.md](README.md).

## QA checklist

- [ ] Every secret in `secrets.txt` is explained here.
- [ ] Rules & Rulings matches `character.yaml`.
- [ ] Current numbers match `ddtools summary`.
